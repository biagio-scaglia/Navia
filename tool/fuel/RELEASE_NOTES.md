Daily fuel prices for the Navia app, rebuilt twice a day by the
`Fuel data` workflow from the open data of the Italian Ministry of
Enterprises and Made in Italy (MIMIT, "Osservaprezzi carburanti").

`fuel-prices.json.gz` lists the fuel stations that reported prices, with
their position, brand, name, address and the prices of each fuel, self
service or served, with the time they were reported.
