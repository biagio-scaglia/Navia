Daily fuel prices of Italian fuel stations for the Navia app, rebuilt every
morning by the `Fuel data` workflow.

`fuel-prices.json.gz` joins the station registry and the prices valid at
8:00 published by the Ministero delle Imprese e del Made in Italy
("Osservaprezzi carburanti"). Prices are those reported by the operators to
the Ministry and may differ from the prices at the pump.

<https://www.mimit.gov.it/it/open-data/elenco-dataset/carburanti-prezzi-praticati-e-anagrafica-degli-impianti>,
